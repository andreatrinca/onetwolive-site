Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how the OneTwoLive website uses cookies and similar technologies.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on a user's device when the user visits a website. They can be used for technical purposes, such as enabling basic website functionality, or for other purposes such as analytics, advertising or user profiling.

2. Cookies used by this website

The OneTwoLive website is a static informational website. At the moment, it does not use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, profiling cookies or marketing tracking technologies.

The website may use only technical elements necessary for the correct delivery, security and display of the website pages.

3. Third-party websites and services

The OneTwoLive website contains links to external services, including Microsoft Store and the payment provider used to purchase licenses. When users open those external websites, their own privacy and cookie policies apply.

OneTwoLive does not control cookies or tracking technologies used by third-party websites.

4. Consent

Since this website does not currently use non-technical cookies, analytics cookies, advertising cookies or profiling cookies, no cookie consent is requested through this website.

If analytics, advertising or other non-technical tracking tools are added in the future, this Cookie Policy will be updated and, where required, users will be asked for consent before those tools are activated.

5. Managing cookies in your browser

Users can manage, block or delete cookies through their browser settings. Blocking some technical elements may affect how certain websites work, although this website is designed to remain accessible without optional cookies.

7. Updates

This Cookie Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website or to applicable legal requirements.
